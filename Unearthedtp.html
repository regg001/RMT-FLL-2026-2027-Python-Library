Our team consists of four FLL veterans and four FLL novices that meet together at RoboMindTech Center. We are from many different schools in Bayside, Manhattan, Kew Gardens & Forest Hills, and Long Island.

This season has been a lot of fun - we learned about the STEAM journey together and built robots and the Lego Growth Toad Model. This wonderful experience has allowed us to deepen our friendships, as well as learn and play.

UNEARTHED is a great topic in which we can make a great impact with a sense of community through Archeology. We shared our ideas to kids in RoboMindTech center and other robotics teams.
