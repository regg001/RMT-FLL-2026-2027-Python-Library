UNEARTHED - Team Profile


Team Overview:


Our team consists of four FLL veterans and four FLL rookies that meet together at RoboMindTech Center. We are from many different schools in Bayside, Manhattan, Kew Gardens & Forest Hills, and Long Island. Half of our team has 2-3 years of experience with the FLL Game, with the other half of our memebers having 1. Together as an 8 person team, we collaborate under the guidance of our coachs and mentors throughout the season.
