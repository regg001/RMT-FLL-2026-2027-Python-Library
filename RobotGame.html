Robot Game


Robot Design




Our robot “Binary Bot” was given its name based on our team name, Binary Builders. It utilizes:

  • Rear-wheel drive for better turning and faster speed, while not hindering our control
  • Special mid-Large size LEGO wheels for the best control and speed
  • An outer frame designed to maintain structure and hold wire management
  • Detachable frame allowing for easy maintenance
  • Two front large motors with square gears for attachments
  • Both mechanical and sensor calibration
  • Gear mechanism on our brain system, allowing for easy battery swappage


Our strategy is to prioritize time by clustering missions together based on the type of attachment they needed to complete.

First complete the easy missions that aren't out of the way, and then see what other ones we could fit in. We also considered what types of attachments we would need for each mission, and then considered what attachments would be the most effective.



Our Code


Our robot utilizes Python functions for increased efficiency and precision, using pre-coded functions such as Gyro Straight that accept adjustable parameters, allowing us to control the distance the robot moves, its speed, and its target angle all in one place.

The target angle of our robot operates on a 360-degree coordinate plane, which allows it to always point toward the correct heading.



Unlike typical code, our robot uses a gyro to increase precision and consistency. The gyro measures the robot's movement, and more specifically, yaw measures its horizontal rotation, giving us accurate real-time feedback on where the robot is pointing.

Our robot has parameters that allow it to scale its speed and motor output depending on how close it is to its target. This scaling is done through a proportional relationship: if the robot is more off its target, it corrects itself more, and if it is less off its target, it corrects itself less. This allows the robot to make fast corrections without overshooting its goal, resulting in an extremely strong and reliable gyro system.



Our robot maintains a single fixed zero point on the 360-degree coordinate plane, with all other angles defined relative to that location. This allows the robot to reach any exact angle regardless of where it currently is, while also reducing the amount of math needed to determine the correct heading at any given moment.
